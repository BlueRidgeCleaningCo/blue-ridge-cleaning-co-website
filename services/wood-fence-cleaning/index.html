Wood fences need their own cleaning approach

Natural wood, stained wood and painted fence boards can respond differently to cleaning. Tell us about any coating and visible wear. If your fence is plastic or vinyl, choose our plastic and vinyl fence cleaning service instead.

What to expect from cleaning

Fence cleaning can lift dirt and organic buildup and improve the appearance of the wood. It does not repair rot, warped boards, loose fasteners or weathered finishes. Stain removal, staining and sealing are not part of a standard cleaning quote.

Common questions

Wood Fence Cleaning questions

Do you clean both sides of a wood fence?

Both sides can be quoted when access and any necessary permission are available. Tell us which sides and gates you want included.

Will cleaning make an old fence look new?

Cleaning can improve its appearance, but it cannot reverse rot, cracking, weathering or permanent discoloration. We discuss realistic results before scheduling.

Can you clean a stained wood fence?

We evaluate the finish first and choose a method for its condition. Cleaning is separate from removing stain or applying a new coating.
