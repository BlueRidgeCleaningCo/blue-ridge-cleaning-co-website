Plastic and vinyl are different from wood

Vinyl fencing is commonly made from PVC plastic. It needs a cleaning approach suited to its surface and condition. Our wood fence cleaning service separately covers natural, stained and painted wood fences.

What to expect from cleaning

Cleaning can refresh the surface and remove common grime. Sun fading, oxidation, scratches, rust marks and permanent discoloration may remain or require separate evaluation. Washing does not repair cracked panels or damaged fittings.

Common questions

Plastic & Vinyl Fence Cleaning questions

Do you clean white vinyl fences?

Yes. We evaluate white vinyl fence panels, posts and gates for dirt and organic buildup and confirm the included areas in your quote.

Can you remove green algae and dark buildup?

Our cleaning targets common organic buildup and surface grime. Permanent staining, oxidation and weathering may not be removable with standard cleaning.

Can both sides and the gates be included?

Yes, when access and permission allow. Include the approximate fence length, the sides to be cleaned and the number of gates when requesting a quote.
